    InboxKey

    Why this exists

    I built InboxKey because I was tired of switching between tabs to copy verification codes. It sounds small, but it interrupts your flow dozens of times a week.

    Email access is sensitive. I know that. That is exactly why everything is open-source, runs locally, and will never phone home. You should not have to trust a stranger with your inbox. You should be able to verify.

    Selman, the dev
